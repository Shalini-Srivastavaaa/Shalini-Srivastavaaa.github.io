About Me

For 3+ years I've worked directly with founders and teams in the US, EU and India to get AI working in production: RAG pipelines, voice agents, multi-agent workflows and search, across legal tech, e-commerce, real estate and media.

Most came with a problem, not a spec.

I worked out what the business actually needed, designed the system, built it, and stayed on it until it ran.

I don't call anything accurate until I've tested it. That has meant 97% query accuracy on a 127,000-document legal search index, 95.3% retrieval accuracy on a support-ticket RAG pipeline measured against a 200-query eval set, and a voice agent built on retrieval so it doesn't pay to reprocess every document on every call.

Underneath the LLM work is a solid ML foundation: classical models, neural networks and training my own models. That's what lets me reason about why a system misbehaves instead of just restarting it.

Now I want to own systems end to end as part of one team, full-time, for the long term.

Tool-Call Verifier

ML · Agent Reliability

A small transformer I trained to sit between an LLM agent and its tools. It reads the conversation and scores each tool call before it runs, so a wrong tool, a bad argument or a broken constraint can be stopped before it causes a failure. Its 8-class failure taxonomy was built from real τ²-bench failures, and it's benchmarked against a Gemini LLM judge on a frozen 116-example set.

PyTorch Transformers τ²-bench Python
